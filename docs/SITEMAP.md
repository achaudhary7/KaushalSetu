## robots.txt policy

**Disallow:** `/dashboard/`, `/admin/`, `/settings/`, `/api/`, `/auth/`, `/onboarding/`,
`/messages/`, `/verify/` (individual codes), and high-cardinality filter permutations on
`/opportunities` beyond the primary facets.

**Allow everything else**, and declare the sitemap absolutely.

Remember: `Disallow` blocks *crawling*, not indexing. Anything that must stay out of the index needs
a `noindex` on a page Google is permitted to crawl.
